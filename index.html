OUR STORY

About SPACS

The Students Programming Association of Computer Science (SPACS) is a dynamic student-led organization at the forefront of technical education and innovation.

Programming Contests

Hosts engaging computer programming contests to sharpen logical thinking and competitive coding abilities.

Seminars & Workshops

Organizes workshops on modern frameworks and IT subjects, keeping students updated with industrial tech.

Leadership & Management

Encourages the development of organizational, managerial, and team coordination skills through student-led events.

Our Core Mission

To bridge the gap between academic programming theory and actual industry standards, molding students into high-quality technical contributors and managers.

  • 100% Peer-to-Peer Learning Environment
  • Weekly Interactive Coding Competitions
  • Expert Guest Lectures & Alumni Mentoring

What We Do

4 pillars that form the foundation of every SPACS initiative and event.

Programming Contests

FIRST-YEAR STUDENT INDUCTION

SPACS Orientation Program

13th August, 2026
College Auditorium
1:00 PM – 3:00 PM
Anita Miss, Lasin & Aishwarya

Organized for newly joined first-year students to welcome them to the college community and introduce them to the SPACS ecosystem. The program kicked off with an inaugural address by Teacher Coordinator Anita Miss, followed by an overview of activities and skill pathways presented by Lasin and Aishwarya. The interactive session featured icebreaker games including Complete the Lyrics, Spin & Balance, Guess the Situation, and Speed Number. The gathering concluded with a formal Vote of Thanks and an official group photograph.

COLLABORATIVE 3-DAY COMPETITION

Internal Smart India Hackathon 2026

1 – 3 September, 2026 (3 Days)
SDPK Hall, CEC
SPACS CEC × IEEE SB CEC × IEDC CEC
21 Teams • 126 Student Engineers

Organised in tri-collaboration with IEEE SB CEC and IEDC CEC, this flagship three-day hackathon challenged 21 multidisciplinary teams (126 students) spanning CSE, AI&DS, ECE, and EEE. Participants engineered technical solutions to complex real-world challenges through continuous mentoring, architecture design, and iterative code execution. The event fostered exceptional innovation, cross-department collaboration, and pitch skills.

#03 AI WORKSHOP
View Full Photo
GENERATIVE AI & WEB DEVELOPMENT

AI Tools & Prompt Engineering Workshop

18th September, 2026
Lab, CS Block, CEC
2:00 PM – 4:00 PM
Speaker: Akhil Jose

Initiated with an opening welcome by Aiswarya Chandran, SPACS Event Management Head, this hands-on workshop explored modern Artificial Intelligence capabilities and professional prompt engineering. Speaker Akhil Jose emphasized the mechanics of role-based framing, context scoping, and structural constraints. In the interactive lab session, students utilized Claude AI to engineer a personal responsive portfolio website from scratch, acquiring first-hand proficiency in AI-accelerated frontend development.

Claude AI Prompt Engineering Portfolio Generation Hands-on Lab
#04 BOOTCAMP
View Full Photo
TECHNICAL FOUNDATIONS FOR FIRST-YEARS

Python Basics & Intro to Machine Learning

5th September, 2026 (Full Day)
Lab at CEC CS Block
9:00 AM – 4:00 PM
Speakers: Joann Elizabeth & Jibin Krishna (S7)

Organized specifically for junior engineers to cultivate early algorithmic coding fluency. Led by senior students Joann Elizabeth and Jibin Krishna (SPACS Chair), 40 students registered with 30 students participating actively in hands-on lab exercises. Attendees gained practical experience writing and running scripts on Google Colab, followed by an introduction to foundational Machine Learning models and their industry use cases.

#05 INTERNSHIP
View Full Photo
FLAGSHIP SKILL ACCELERATOR

10-Day React Native Internship – Day 1 Kickoff

25th September, 2026
SDPK Hall
2:00 PM – 4:00 PM
Session Lead: Irishikesh (S7 CSB)

The inaugural day of the ambitious 10-Day React Native Mobile Development Internship gathered 35 registered attendees supported by 12 dedicated student volunteers. Under the instruction of Irishikesh (S7 CSB), the session concentrated on calibrating local developer environments: configuring Node.js runtimes, package managers, and MySQL instances. This foundation prepared participants with the developer tooling required for progressive cross-platform mobile application development.

Community Scale: 35 Student Developers Mentored by 12 SPACS Volunteers
